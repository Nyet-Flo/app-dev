## What is the series about?
*Vincenzo* is a 2021 South Korean dark comedy and crime drama series. It follows Vincenzo Cassano, a ==Korean-born lawyer who was adopted by an Italian mafia family==. After a betrayal within the mafia, he returns to Seoul to recover a hidden stash of gold beneath a building. However, he becomes entangled in a battle against a corrupt conglomerate, Babel Group, and teams up with lawyer Hong Cha-young to fight for justice using both legal strategies and mafia tactics. 

The series blends action, humor, and drama, offering a unique take on the anti-hero narrative. It gained significant popularity and is available for streaming on Netflix.

### Casts of Vincenzo
-Vincenzo Cassano (Song Joong-ki) – A suave Korean-Italian mafia consigliere and lawyer who returns to Korea to recover hidden gold and ends up fighting corruption.

-Hong Cha-young (Jeon Yeo-been) – A sharp and quirky lawyer who initially works for a corrupt firm but later joins Vincenzo in his fight against Babel Group.

-Jang Jun-woo / Jang Han-seok (Ok Taec-yeon) – The seemingly goofy intern who is secretly the ruthless chairman of Babel Group.

-Choi Myung-hee (Kim Yeo-jin) – A manipulative former prosecutor and Babel’s top legal strategist.

-Jang Han-seo (Kwak Dong-yeon) – Jun-woo’s younger brother, used as a puppet leader of Babel; he has a complex relationship with power and family.

-Nam Joo-sung (Yoo Jae-myung) – Loyal paralegal who supports Vincenzo and Cha-young.

-Geumga Plaza Tenants – A quirky but lovable group of residents who eventually team up with Vincenzo. Notable ones include:

Tak Hong-shik (tailor)

Seo Mi-ri (pianist with secrets)

Chef Toto

Larry Kang (wannabe actor)
